## Facts checked against Razorpay docs (PLAYBOOK step 7), 2026-10-30

- Subscription events: `subscription.authenticated`, `.activated`, `.charged`, `.completed`, `.updated`, `.pending` (charge failed, Razorpay retrying), `.halted` (retries exhausted), `.cancelled`, `.paused`, `.resumed`. Payload carries `current_start`, `current_end`, `charge_at`, `status`, `paid_count`, plus a `payment` entity when a payment preceded the event.
- Headers: `X-Razorpay-Signature` (HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body with the webhook secret) and `x-razorpay-event-id` (unique per event, documented for duplicate detection).
- Razorpay states that the same event may be delivered more than once and that events are not guaranteed to arrive in order.
- Not found in the pages read: the exact retry duration for failed deliveries (the spec's "24 h" is unverified) and a response timeout for the endpoint.
